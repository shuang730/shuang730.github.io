About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. David Snoke's research group at the University of Pittsburgh, working on semiconductor optics and exciton-polariton condensates.

I received my Ph.D. in Optics from East China Normal University in 2024, where I studied perovskite microcavities, ultrafast lasing, and structured optical emission at the State Key Laboratory of Precision Spectroscopy.

My work combines optical system design and integration with time-, spatially, and angle-resolved spectroscopy. I build and troubleshoot experiments, characterize photonic devices, and develop MATLAB tools for measurement analysis and optical layout planning.

Interested in industry R&D opportunities in optical instrumentation, laser systems, and photonic device characterization.
